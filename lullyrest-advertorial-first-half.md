## 2. A CONTOUR THAT ACTUALLY ACCOUNTS FOR TWO SLEEPING POSITIONS

When you're on your back, your head needs to sit about 1.5 to 2 inches lower than your neck so your cervical spine holds its natural curve. When you're on your side, your head needs to be elevated 4.5 to 5.5 inches to bridge the gap between your ear and shoulder.

So LullyRest uses a 4-zone core: a cervical extension roll that cradles the neck arch, a recessed center cavity that lets the back of the skull sit lower during back sleeping, and elevated lateral wings for side sleeping. Each zone has a specific height calibrated for spinal neutral in that position, so your neck doesn't buckle when you roll over.

## 5. TARGETS THE #1 MECHANICAL CAUSE OF MORNING MIGRAINES

Most pillows claim to "reduce neck pain." LullyRest is built for something more specific: decompressing the C1–C3 nerve roots at the craniocervical junction. Where your neck meets the base of your skull.

When your pillow lets your head drop forward or bend sideways for 8 hours, the small muscles at the skull base clamp down to protect your spinal cord. They squeeze directly onto your occipital nerves. Those nerves feed into the same part of the brain as the nerve that controls sensation across your face, forehead, and eyes. That's why a pinch in your neck shows up as throbbing behind your eye socket, tightness across your forehead, and nausea at 3 AM.
