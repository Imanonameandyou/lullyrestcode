**Identity:** High-performing knowledge workers and corporate professionals juggling career intensity and domestic life while suffering chronic postural strain and screen-induced craniocervical tension.

## Core Pain Points

1. **Nocturnal Nerve Compression & Morning Pain Dread** — waking with a locked cervical column, base-of-skull burning, radiating orbital pressure; 3AM wake-ups with unilateral cervicogenic headaches, dizziness, nausea/vomiting.
2. **Workplace Cognitive Impairment** — afternoon brain fog, word-retrieval failures in high-stakes moments, inability to sit at a dual-monitor workstation, burning through sick leave.
3. **"Pillow Graveyard" Fatigue** — 8–15 discarded pillows, $500+ spent, chemical off-gassing (VOC) triggering migraines, rigid contours crushing ear cartilage (chondritis/TMJ).

## Goals

- **Short-term:** wake pain-free, sustain 8-hour cognitive clarity, preserve daytime PT/dry-needling gains overnight.
- **Long-term:** be fully present with spouse/kids instead of retreating to a dark room; travel without hotel-pillow fear; drop dependence on abortives/muscle relaxants/injections in favor of anatomical fix.

## Psychographics

- **Mechanical problem-solver** — believes structural compression needs a structural fix, not a pharmaceutical band-aid.
- **Skeptical, analytical buyer** — dismisses generic "plush comfort" claims; wants biomechanical rationale (C1–C3 unloading, dual-plane loft) and risk-reversal trials.
- **Hyper-sensitive sensory profile** — vulnerable to VOC odors and excessive firmness.
- **Disillusioned with mainstream medicine** — tired of NSAIDs/triptans/muscle relaxants that mask rather than fix.

## Emotional Journey

Awareness (dismiss as fatigue/stress) → Frustration (doctors + retail pillows + PT fail) → Desperation (pillow graveyard, root-cause research) → Relief & Commitment (learns dual-plane mechanism, commits to 60-night trial, achieves pain-free mornings).

## Objections to Pre-empt

| Objection | Root fear | Counter |
|---|---|---|
| "I own 10 failed pillows already" | Wasting more money, another graveyard entry | Explain single-height design flaw vs. dual-plane zoning |
| "Memory foam gives me instant migraines" | VOC sensitivity | Zero-VOC vacuum-baking, medical-grade certification |
| "Looks too hard, will hurt ears/jaw" | Chondritis/TMJ pain | Ear cavities + dynamic viscoelastic rebound |
| "My condition is too severe/unique" | Product won't work for me specifically | 60-night in-home adaptation trial, zero financial risk |
