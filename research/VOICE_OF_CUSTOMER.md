# LullyRest — Voice-of-Customer Swipe File

Raw quotes pulled from patient communities, chronic pain forums, and product reviews (see `source-docs/Cervical Pillow Market Dossier.docx` for full sourcing/citations). Use verbatim or near-verbatim in ad hooks, headlines, and testimoniable language — this is how the audience actually talks about the problem.

## Morning Awakening

> "I get these headaches that start in my neck in the morning when I wake up. I feel the stiffness in my neck, and slowly throughout the morning it travels over my forehead into my eyebrow and eyes."

> "I wake up feeling like a heavy iron clamp was tightened around the base of my skull all night, and my neck is completely frozen to one side."

> "Woke up at 3:30 AM nauseated from the sheer intensity of the burning nerve pain running from my upper neck directly behind my right eyeball."

> "The pain spreads everywhere in my head, my neck locks up, I get pain behind my eyes, nausea, and sometimes I even vomit. The strangest part is that I cannot lay down when I have them."

> "I have the most intense stiff neck, and the urge to crack it. Tingling in the back of my head and base of my skull, a lot of pain/pressure at the base of my skull, fuzzy head feeling, pressure in my ears."

## Workplace & Productivity

> "I cannot sit at a computer for more than 10 minutes. The tension/tight feeling is so bad it makes my arms feel physically weak and a trembling sensation. I get very lightheaded."

> "I'm absentminded, forgetful, and I'm losing words in the middle of client presentations because my neck has been spasming since 6:00 AM."

> "The brain fog is real and sometimes I struggle to truly be present at my job. I get dizzy and slow for 2-3 days... it's quite noticeable because I'd take longer to respond to situations compared to my peers."

> "I have to dim my monitors to the lowest brightness setting and wear blue-light glasses just to make it through a basic spreadsheet without throwing up."

> "I burned through all my annual sick leave in four months just managing morning neck flare-ups that made it impossible to drive safely."

## Emotional Exhaustion & Relationship Strain

> "If I went to lay in a dark room every time I had a migraine, I couldn't hold a job, have a social life, date, exercise, take care of my cat, etc. it's just not feasible. I lie about how frequent they are so I don't have to explain."

> "I think what most people who don't get migraines don't understand is that it's not just a headache... I get anxiety, fatigue, brain fog, nausea and really struggle to stay focused at work. I feel like it's easy for me to feel like I'm lazy or dumb."

> "I feel like an absent parent because by 6:00 PM I have to lock myself in a dark, quiet bedroom with an ice pack while my partner handles everything alone."

> "You reach a point where you feel like your body has betrayed you, and you start wondering if you'll ever experience a clear, pain-free day again."

> "I am sick of living with shame and having to deal with self-help culture, instead of having access to people who understand how debilitating this is."

## Pillow Shopping Cynicism / "Pillow Graveyard"

> "I literally have a pillow graveyard, I've tried so many different pillows to try to prevent neck pain and pinched nerves. I've tried memory foam, adjustable shredded foam, cervical contour, feather fill, latex."

> "I have what my husband calls the pillow graveyard. I have tried so many. Most don't last more than a week or two."

> "I too have a pillow graveyard. And can someone please make a pillow that doesn't come with an instant migraine scent."

> "Paying a higher price for a pillow does not guarantee you a better pillow... but maybe worst of all was the god-awful memory foam chemical smell that never got better. The scent alone contributed to the migraines."

> "I saved up to spend almost $400 for a pillow from Europe that I immediately gave to my mom. I literally could have cried... But I'll ban myself from pillows entirely if these don't work haha."

> "Every brand advertises an orthopedic contour, but after three weeks the foam collapses in the middle and you're right back to waking up with a stiff neck."

> "I have a closet full of expensive, useless foam shapes that failed to do anything except give me ear pain and take up storage space."

## Horror Stories (longer-form, good for advertorial/story-based copy)

- **The 3AM vomiting flare-up:** sleeping on an unsupportive/rigid pillow → explosive unilateral headache at the craniocervical junction → referred pain triggers vagal nausea → incapacitated on the bathroom floor, unable to lie back down.
- **The hotel pillow vacation derailment:** one night on a soft/unsupportive hotel pillow → multi-day migraine → confined to a darkened hotel room while family goes on without them.
- **The $500+ pillow graveyard:** 8–15 discarded pillows (shredded foam, water bladders, latex, infomercial contour) — each one a burst of hope followed by disappointment.
