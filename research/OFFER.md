## The Big Idea

**"Nocturnal Lordotic Kink" / Dual-Plane Sleeping Mismatch** — daytime therapies (PT, dry needling) fail because 8 hours on a flat, non-zoned pillow forces the cervical spine out of alignment every night, pinching the C1–C3 nerve roots and undoing the day's recovery.

**Metaphor — The Pinched Power Cable:** the neck is a heavy-duty cable routing power into the craniocervical junction box. A bad pillow kinks the cable like a garden hose; pinched "copper wires" (occipital nerves) trip alarm bells in the "main fuse box" (temples, forehead, eyes).
