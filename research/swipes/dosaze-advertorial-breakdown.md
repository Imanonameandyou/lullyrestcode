# Swipe Breakdown — Dosaze "10 Reasons Chiropractors Recommend" Advertorial

Source: `dosaze-10-reasons-chiropractors-advertorial.pdf` (indirect competitor, dosaze.com). Pulled as a native/paid-social pre-sale advertorial swipe.

## Structure (top to bottom)

1. Sticky top bar: sale banner + "SHOP NOW" — advertorial still leaks the store, doesn't fully mask as editorial.
2. Headline: "10 Reasons Chiropractors Recommend The Dosaze Pillow For Neck Pain" — numbered-list + authority-borrow + explicit outcome.
3. Byline: "Dr. Brady W." with a "Verified" badge and avatar — authority persona, not the brand.
4. TL;DR callout box directly under the byline — summarizes the payoff before the scroll (mobile skim optimization).
5. Ten numbered sections, each: bold reason headline + 2–4 sentence body + one supporting image/graphic. One image-and-claim per scroll unit — nothing dense.
6. Reason #6 is the offer itself ("Buy 1, Get 1 Free — This Week Only") embedded as a numbered reason, not broken out separately — offer doesn't interrupt the read, it *is* a reason.
7. Full offer-stack graphic after the list closes: sale badge, guarantee ("Try It Risk Free for 60 Nights"), Free Shipping / Free Returns / Free Exchanges, single CTA button.
8. Simulated comment section: "From readers who tried Dosaze after reading this article" — named commenters, avatars, likes/replies, one reply from the byline author ("Dr. Brady W.") engaging back.
9. Footer, standard.

## Reason-by-reason (what each is doing)

| # | Claim | Persuasion job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stop waking up in pain — sleep like you used to | Loss aversion / nostalgia — "you used to" implies something was taken from them |
| 2 | Contour that actually keeps your neck aligned | Mechanism claim, "actually" implies competitors fail at this |
| 3 | Designed & endorsed by top chiropractors | Third-party authority, real-looking quote from a named chiropractor |
| 4 | Targets the #1 cause of morning neck pain & headaches | Root-cause narrative (their version of our "kink" story) |
| 5 | Relieves pain, reduces snoring & restores energy | Benefit-stacking — broadens beyond core pain claim |
| 6 | BOGO offer, this week only | Offer + urgency, disguised as a "reason" |
| 7 | Two heights (Standard & Thick) | Pre-empts "one size doesn't fit me" — comparison table |
| 8 | Holds loft for years not weeks (CertiPUR-US foam) | Directly answers "foam collapses in 3 weeks" objection, cites a real cert for credibility |
| 9 | Works for side, back & stomach sleepers | Versatility / removes "wrong for my sleep position" objection |
| 10 | 60-night risk-free trial | Risk reversal, closes the list |

## What to steal for LullyRest

- **Numbered-listicle skeleton** for the pre-sale advertorial (OFFER.md funnel step 2) — one claim, one image, one scroll unit, repeat. Native-feeling, mobile-first, low cognitive load per section.
- **TL;DR box under the byline** — give skimmers the payoff immediately; don't make them earn it.
- **Byline as the Guru Persona**, not the brand — our Biomechanical Spine & Sleep Ergonomics Specialist persona (OFFER.md) slots directly into the "Dr. Brady W., Verified" convention.
- **Bury the offer as a numbered reason**, not a separate ad block, so it reads as content instead of interruption.
- **One reason dedicated to directly rebutting the "foam collapses" objection**, with a named material/certification as proof — this is a near-exact structural match for our own "pillow graveyard" objection in AUDIENCE.md/OFFER.md. Should be its own numbered reason for us too, naming our zero-VOC / high-density open-cell foam spec.
- **Simulated comment section after the offer stack** as a closing trust device — normalizes "I read this article and tried it," reinforces bandwagon effect right before the reader would leave.

## What NOT to copy

- **Mechanism claims are shallow** ("keeps your neck aligned," "targets the #1 cause") — no anatomy, no numbers. That works for Dosaze's presumably lower-sophistication audience, but ours is explicitly Stage 4 / "pillow graveyard fatigue" (OFFER.md) — they've heard vague alignment claims before and distrust them. Our reasons need the C1–C3 / lordotic-angle specificity the belief chain requires, not Dosaze's generic version.
- **Snoring/energy benefit-stack (#5)** risks diluting our sharper, more emotionally potent migraine/nerve-compression narrative — fine as a minor mention, not a headline reason.
- **TL;DR copy has a grammar error** ("10x benefits that's non regular pillows can't hold for you") — sloppy; don't let skim-optimization override basic proofreading.
- **BOGO as the offer mechanic** — not confirmed as our model; OFFER.md's stack is core unit + ThermaFlow cover + digital bonus + guarantee, no BOGO. Don't inherit their offer structure without a deliberate decision.
